IN PLAIN WORDS

The VPC is your secured campus. EC2 A is the reception desk in the public area; EC2 B is a restricted room with no direct public entrance.

TECHNICAL EXPLANATION

In us-east-1, place the bastion in a public subnet and the target in a private subnet. The bastion needs a route to the target, and EC2 B must accept SSH from the bastion security group.
